
## 1. The Core Problem

After 28 live trades, the system is barely breakeven.  
The single biggest reason: **it trades every day the same way, even when there’s no momentum to capture.**

- June 29 (a choppy, range‑bound Monday) killed –₹4,068 with an 80% loss rate.
- June 30 (expiry trend day) recovered +₹4,378 with a 50% win rate.

**The system only works on Trend / Momentum‑Burst days.  
On all other days, it should not trade — and now it won’t.**

## 2. The Two‑Layer Solution

```
MARKET TYPE DETECTION (Layer 1)
         ↓
  "Is momentum working today?"
         ↓
  YES → Trade with confidence
  NO  → Skip the session entirely
         ↓
SIGNAL‑LEVEL FILTERS (Layer 2)
         ↓
EXECUTION & RISK MANAGEMENT
         ↓
PROFIT MANAGEMENT
```

---

## 3. Layer 1 – Market Type Detection (The Gatekeeper)

*All indicators computed locally from Upstox raw candles via `pandas-ta`.  
Decision by 10:15 IST, re‑assessed every 15 minutes.*

| Regime | Detection | Action |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| 🟢 **Trend** | ADX(15m) > 25 **and** Trend Persistence ≥ 20 min **and** VWAP uncrossed for 30 min; ORB breakout holds | Full size, +25% after first win, hold runner to T2 |
| 🟡 **Momentum Burst** | 18 ≤ ADX ≤ 25 **or** ADX > 25 but Trend Persistence < 20 min; VWAP crossed 2‑3× | Trade only high‑confidence signals. Book 100% at T1. Tight stops. |
| 🟠 **Range** | ADX < 18 for 1h **or** ORB intact **or** Breakout Success Rate ≤ 30% | **🚫 No momentum trades allowed.** |
| 🔴 **Whipsaw** | Large two‑way candles, ADX high but DI+/DI‑ crossing, VWAP crossed >4×, 2+ failed breakouts | **🚫 No momentum trades allowed** (or drastically reduced: 0.4× size, only if confidence > 75 + 2 confirmers) |

**On Range and Whipsaw days, the system simply does not trade.**  
This isn’t a failure — it’s the smartest possible action. Avoiding a –4% day is mathematically identical to making +4%.

## 4. Layer 2 – Entry Filters (Only on Trade‑Allowed Days)

| Filter | Rule |
|--------|------|
| `ba_press` cap | Skip if contribution > 80% unless 2+ other confirmers |
| Trend alignment | Long: `ema_cross_pct > 0` & `mid_price > vwap`; Short: reverse |
| Smart RSI | Skip long only if RSI14 > 75 **and** price > VWAP*1.005 **and** ATR falling |
| Spread filter | Skip if `(spread / mid_price) > 0.015` |
| Deep ITM expiry ban | Skip if delta > 0.95 on expiry day |
| ORB gate (time‑bound) | Before 10:30: long only above ORB high, short only below ORB low. After 10:30: ignore ORB. |
| Multi‑timeframe | 15‑min candle must agree (close > open for long) |
| Signal decay | Cancel if signal older than 30 seconds |

## 5. Sizing, Execution, Profit Management

- **Base risk:** 1% of account per trade.
- **Scaling:** VIX, regime, day‑of‑week, time‑of‑day, consecutive‑loss reductions applied sequentially.
- **Stop‑limit orders** with 1% limit, ATR‑based stops, GTT disaster stop.
- **Partial exit** (50% at T1), trailing stop from 0.75R, hard time stop.

---

## 6. What About Range / Whipsaw Days? – The Full Answer

Your system is a **pure momentum breakout strategy**.  
Its edge exists *only* when the market is trending or delivering clean momentum bursts.  

**On Range or Whipsaw days, the edge disappears completely.**  
Forcing it to trade on those days is what caused the –₹4,068 loss.

The new strategy therefore:

- **Detects those days early** (by 10:15 AM) using ADX, Trend Persistence, and Breakout Success Rate.
- **Shuts down for the session** (or trades at a tiny fraction of size if you insist).
- **Preserves capital** for the next Trend day.

This single change — knowing when to sit out — is the biggest single profit‑driver in the entire plan.

If you later want to attack Range days, you’d build a **separate mean‑reversion module**, not try to force a momentum system to work where it can’t. But that’s entirely optional; many professional traders simply wait for the next trend.
