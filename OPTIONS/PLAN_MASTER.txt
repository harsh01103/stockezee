## 1. The Core Problem

After 28 live trades, the original momentum‑only system was barely breakeven (+₹309).  
Root causes:

- Trades every day identically, even when momentum is absent.
- Over‑dependence on a single noisy signal (`ba_press`).
- Brutal stop‑loss slippage (up to –13.9 pts).
- No profit management.
- Erratic risk sizing.

**The system only works on Trend / Momentum‑Burst days. On Range or Whipsaw days it destroys capital.**  
The solution is twofold:  
1. A real‑time regime detector that knows exactly what kind of market we are in.  
2. A second strategy for mean‑reverting Range days, and a safety mode for Whipsaw days.

---

## 2. System Architecture

```
Every 15 minutes (starting at 10:15 IST)
        │
        ▼
MARKET TYPE DETECTOR
(ADX, Trend Persistence, Breakout Success Rate, VWAP crossings)
        │
        ├── REGIME = TREND ──────────► Activate Strategy A: Momentum Breakout
        │
        ├── REGIME = MOMENTUM BURST ─► Activate Strategy A with reduced aggression
        │
        ├── REGIME = RANGE ──────────► Activate Strategy B: Mean‑Reversion (Fade Extremes)
        │
        └── REGIME = WHIPSAW ────────► Activate Safety Mode (no trades, or 0.4× size only if extreme confidence)
```

The detector runs continuously. If the regime changes during the day, the active strategy switches automatically.

## 3. Market Type Detection (The Brain)

All indicators are computed locally from Upstox raw candles (1‑min, 5‑min, 15‑min) using `pandas‑ta`.  
Detection runs at **10:15 IST** and then every **15 minutes** thereafter.

### 3.1 Key Metrics

| Metric | Calculation | Purpose |
|--------|-------------|---------|
| **ADX(15)** | `pandas_ta.adx(high, low, close, length=14)` on 15‑min candles | Strength of trend (direction agnostic) |
| **Trend Persistence** | Count of consecutive 5‑min candles closing above 9‑EMA (for longs) without crossing VWAP. If ≥4 candles (≈20 min), momentum is persistent. | Filters out choppy ADX >25 |
| **Breakout Success Rate** | Track first 5 ORB breakout attempts. Success = price moves outside ORB and stays there ≥5 min. If ≤1 success → momentum is failing. | Direct measure of breakout reliability |
| **VWAP Cross Counter** | Count how many times price crossed VWAP in last 1 hour. | High crosses → range / whipsaw |
| **ATR(14, 5min)** | `pandas_ta.atr()` on 5‑min candles | Volatility context |
| **ORB (Opening Range)** | High and Low of 9:15–9:45 IST from 1‑min candles | Range boundaries |

### 3.2 Regime Decision Tree

```
IF ADX > 25
    AND Trend Persistence ≥ 20 min
    AND VWAP uncrossed for 30 min
    → 🟢 TREND

ELSE IF ADX ≥ 18 (or ADX >25 but Trend Persistence <20 min)
    AND VWAP crossed 2‑3 times in last hour
    → 🟡 MOMENTUM BURST

ELSE IF ADX < 18 for 1 hour
    OR (Breakout Success Rate ≤ 30% AND ORB intact)
    OR VWAP crossed >3 times and ATR not expanding
    → 🟠 RANGE

ELSE
    → 🔴 WHIPSAW (high ADX but DI+/DI- crossing, large two‑way candles, or 2+ failed breakouts with long wicks)
```

**Note:** On **expiry day**, after 14:00 IST, automatically downgrade Trend → Momentum Burst and Range → Whipsaw (due to late‑day volatility spikes).

## 4. Strategy A – Momentum Breakout (For Trend & Momentum Burst Regimes)

This is the refined version of your original system, now with all improvements.

### 4.1 Entry Filters (applied in order)

| # | Filter | Rule |
|---|--------|------|
| 1 | `ba_press` cap | Skip if `ba_press` contribution >80% unless ≥2 of `vol_burst`, `ema_cross`, `oi_accel` confirm direction. |
| 2 | Trend alignment | Long: `ema_cross_pct > 0` **and** `mid_price > vwap`; Short: reverse. |
| 3 | Smart RSI | Skip long only if RSI14 > 75 **and** price > VWAP×1.005 **and** ATR falling. |
| 4 | Spread filter | Skip if `(bidAskSpread / mid_price) > 0.015`. |
| 5 | Deep ITM expiry ban | Skip if `delta > 0.95` on expiry day. |
| 6 | ORB gate (time‑bound) | Before 10:30: long only if spot > ORB high; short only if < ORB low. After 10:30: ignore ORB. |
| 7 | Multi‑TF confirmation | 15‑min candle must agree (close > open for long). |
| 8 | Signal decay | Cancel pending order if `(now – signal_timestamp) > 30s`. |

### 4.2 Position Sizing (Strategy A)

Base risk = 1% of account.  
`qty = floor( (account * 0.01) / (entry – stop_loss) / lotSize ) * lotSize`

Multipliers (applied sequentially):
- VIX: `× (15 / india_vix)`
- Regime: Trend ×1.0, Momentum Burst ×0.75
- Day of week: Mon/Fri ×0.75
- Time of day: 12:00–13:30 ×0.5
- After 3 consecutive losses: ×0.5 until a win.

Max 2 concurrent positions, never same underlying.

### 4.3 Execution & Profit Management

- **Stop‑limit orders**: SL‑Limit with limit 1% beyond trigger. Cancel if unfilled in 3 sec.
- **ATR‑based stops**: `1.5 × ATR(14, 5min)` distance.
- **GTT disaster stop**: 20% away placed immediately.
- **Partial exit**: 50% at T1, move SL to breakeven on remainder.
- **Trailing stop**: Activate at 0.75R profit, trail by 0.25R (or ATR).
- **Hard time stop**: Exit if open > `holdTime` max.

## 5. Strategy B – Mean‑Reversion (For Range Regime)

This strategy is only active when the market is confirmed as Range.

### 5.1 Core Idea

Fade the extremes of the Opening Range. When price touches the top of the range (ORB High) and shows exhaustion, sell (or buy a Put). When it touches the bottom (ORB Low) and shows exhaustion, buy (or buy a Call).

### 5.2 Entry Rules

| Component | Long (Buy) | Short (Sell) |
|-----------|------------|--------------|
| Trigger | Price ≤ ORB Low × 1.002 (within 0.2%) | Price ≥ ORB High × 0.998 (within 0.2%) |
| RSI | RSI14 < 30 | RSI14 > 70 |
| VWAP extension | `VWAP / price – 1 > 0.3%` (price below VWAP) | `price / VWAP – 1 > 0.3%` (price above VWAP) |
| Order type | Limit order at ORB Low (or slightly above) | Limit order at ORB High (or slightly below) |
| Time gate | Only after 10:30 AM (let range establish) | Same |

All conditions must be met. If price breaks through the ORB boundary by more than 0.3%, the signal is invalidated (the range may be breaking).

### 5.3 Exit Rules

| Component | Rule |
|-----------|------|
| Target | Return to VWAP (or fixed 0.6R reward) |
| Stop‑Loss | Beyond the range boundary: `ORB High + 0.2%` (for shorts), `ORB Low – 0.2%` (for longs) |
| Time Stop | Exit if trade open > 20 min without hitting target or stop |
| Partial exit | 50% at VWAP, rest at the opposite ORB boundary (if it reaches) |

### 5.4 Sizing & Risk (Strategy B)

- Risk per trade = **5 to 10%** of account (lower than momentum, because win rate is higher but R‑multiples are smaller).
- Max 1 position at a time (no concurrent positions in range mode).
- Same stop‑limit, ATR‑stop, and GTT disaster stop rules apply.

## 6. Safety Mode – Whipsaw Handling

When the regime is **Whipsaw**, the market is too erratic for either strategy.  
**Default action:** No new trades.  
Optional: If you must trade, only take extremely high‑confidence signals (opportunity_score > 75 and at least 3 features confirming) at **40% of normal size** for momentum, or **20% size** for mean‑reversion. But the recommended professional approach is to **stay flat**.

---

## 7. Unified Risk Management

These rules apply globally, regardless of which strategy is active.

- **Daily loss limit**: Stop trading after –3% of day’s starting balance.
- **Daily profit lock**: At +3%, move loss limit to –1%. At +5%, close all and stop for the day.
- **Consecutive loss scaling**: After 3 losses in a row, reduce size by 50% until a win.
- **All exits via stop‑limit**, never market orders.
- **GTT disaster stop** on every position.
