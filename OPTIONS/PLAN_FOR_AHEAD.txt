## 3. Layer 1 – Market Type Detection

### 3.1 The Four Regimes

All indicators are computed **locally** from Upstox raw candles (1‑min / 5‑min) using `pandas-ta`.  
The regime is decided by **10:15 IST** and re‑assessed every 15 minutes.

| Regime | How to Detect | Action |
|--------|---------------|--------|
| 🟢 **Trend** | `ADX(15m) > 25` **and** `Trend Persistence ≥ 20 min` **and** price stays on one side of VWAP for 30 min; ORB breakout holds | Trade all signals. +25% size after first win. Hold runner to T2. |
| 🟡 **Momentum Burst** | `18 ≤ ADX ≤ 25` **or** `ADX > 25` but Trend Persistence < 20 min; VWAP crossed 2‑3×; ATR normal | Trade only high‑confidence signals (opportunity_score > 70). Book 100% at T1. Tight stops (0.75R). |
| 🟠 **Range** | `ADX < 18` for 1h **or** ORB intact **or** `Breakout Success Rate ≤ 30%` (2+ failures out of first 5 attempts) | **Skip all momentum trades** |
| 🔴 **Whipsaw** | Large two‑way candles (>1.5× ATR), ADX high but DI+/DI‑ crossing, VWAP crossed >4×, **or** 2+ failed breakouts with long wicks | Trade only if opportunity_score > 75 + at least 2 other confirming features. Size = 40% of normal. No T2 hold. |

### 3.2 New Metrics – Computed Locally

**Trend Persistence**  
Count consecutive 5‑min candles that close above the 9‑EMA (for a long bias) without breaking VWAP.  
If this count ≥ 4 (≈20 minutes), momentum is persistent.  
*Builds directly from 5‑min Upstox candles.*

**Breakout Success Rate**  
Track the first 5 ORB breakout attempts of the day. A “success” is when price moves outside the ORB and stays there for ≥5 minutes. If ≤1 success out of 5, the market is rejecting momentum → treat as Range.

### 3.3 Indicator Construction from Upstox Data

- **VWAP**: `cumulative(Close * Volume) / cumulative(Volume)` from 1‑min candles since 9:15.
- **ADX / ATR**: `pandas_ta.adx()` and `pandas_ta.atr()` on a 15‑min DataFrame (for ADX) and 5‑min DataFrame (for ATR).
- **ORB**: Record the High and Low of the first 30 minutes (9:15–9:45) from 1‑min or 5‑min candles.
- **Trend Persistence**: Custom counter on 5‑min candles vs 9‑EMA (computed via `pandas_ta.ema()`).
- **Breakout Success Rate**: Monitored live using ORB and price data.

*All calculations happen inside your Python script – no external service needed.*

## 4. Layer 2 – Signal‑Level Filters (Entry Gate)

Every trade (even on a Trend day) must pass these checks **in this order**.

| # | Filter | Rule | Rationale |
|---|--------|------|-----------|
| 1 | `ba_press` cap | If `ba_press` contribution > 80% of composite score, skip **unless** ≥2 of `vol_burst`, `ema_cross`, `oi_accel` confirm direction. | Kills the most dangerous signal type. |
| 2 | Trend alignment | Long: `ema_cross_pct > 0` **and** `mid_price > vwap`; Short: reverse. | Basic structural filter. |
| 3 | RSI (smart) | Skip long only if `RSI14 > 75` **and** `price > VWAP * 1.005` **and** ATR is falling. | Strong trends can stay overbought; only reject when momentum is exhausted. |
| 4 | Spread filter | `(bidAskSpread / mid_price) > 0.015` → skip trade or reduce size by 50%. | Avoid illiquid strikes. |
| 5 | Deep ITM expiry ban | On expiry day, skip any option with `delta > 0.95`. | Prevents massive gap risk. |
| 6 | ORB gate (time‑bound) | **Before 10:30 IST**: Long only if spot > ORB high; Short only if < ORB low. **After 10:30**: ignore ORB completely. | Protects the opening hour; doesn’t kill afternoon trends. |
| 7 | Multi‑timeframe | 15‑min candle must agree: for longs, close > open and close > candle midpoint; for shorts, opposite. | Simple, robust confirmation. |
| 8 | Signal decay | Cancel any pending order if `(now – signalTimestamp) > 30 seconds`. | Avoids stale entries. |

*No GEX, Max Pain, or hard lunch‑block in this list.*  
Lunch handling is part of sizing, not a trade filter.

## 5. Position Sizing

**Base risk** = 1% of current account equity.  

```
qty = floor( (account * 0.01) / (entry – stop_loss) / lotSize ) * lotSize
```

**Adjustments (multiplied sequentially after base qty):**

1. **VIX scaling**: `qty = qty * (15 / india_vix)`
2. **Regime scaling**:
   - Trend: ×1.0
   - Momentum Burst: ×0.75
   - Range: no trade
   - Whipsaw: ×0.4
3. **Day‑of‑week scaling** (Mon/Fri): ×0.75
4. **Time‑of‑day scaling** (12:00–13:30): ×0.5
5. **Consecutive loss scaling**: after 3 consecutive losses, halve size until a win.
6. **Max concurrent positions**: 2, never on the same underlying.

*All scaling factors are safety nets, not over‑optimizations.*

## 6. Stop‑Loss & Execution

- **Stop‑Limit orders** (replace market SL). Limit = trigger ± 1%. Example: SL 160.45 → sell limit 158.85. Cancel and re‑evaluate if not filled in 3 seconds.
- **ATR‑based stops**: Use `1.5 × ATR(14, 5min)` as the stop distance, placed via stop‑limit.
- **GTT disaster stop**: Place a GTT order 20% away from entry immediately after fill. Cancel on normal exit.

---

## 7. Profit Management

- **Partial exit**: Close 50% of position at Target 1. Move stop to breakeven on the remaining 50%.
- **Trailing stop**: Activate when price reaches 0.75R profit. Trail by 0.25R (or dynamically using ATR).
- **Hard time stop**: If trade is open longer than `holdTime` max and neither T1 nor SL hit, exit at market.

---

## 8. Session Risk Controls

- **Daily loss limit**: Stop trading after a –3% drop from the day’s starting balance.
- **Daily profit lock**: When daily net P&L reaches +3%, tighten loss limit to –1%. At +5%, close all positions and stop trading for the day.
- All stops are placed as **stop‑limit**, not market.
