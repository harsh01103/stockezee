

## 1. The Core Idea

A **hierarchical decision tree** replaces the long filter list:

```
Market Regime (intelligent)
      ↓
Strategy (momentum OR range)
      ↓
Original Signal (trusted)
      ↓
Sanity checks (only 3–4)
      ↓
Trade
```

The regime detector does the heavy lifting.  
Once it says *“this is a good momentum environment”*, the original momentum signal is trusted almost as‑is.

## 3. System Health Monitor

**Before any trade:** verify:

- WebSocket connected & ticks fresh (<3s)
- Candle data complete
- Broker API healthy
- Clock synced
- Greeks available
- Positions reconciled

If any check fails → **SAFE MODE** (no new orders, manage existing).

## 4. Regime Detector (The Brain)

### 4.1 Evaluation Cadence

- Indicators update every minute.
- Regime recalculated **only on 5‑minute candle close**.
- A switch requires **2 consecutive 5‑min evaluations** of the same new regime.

### 4.2 Four Regimes

| Regime | Conditions (all required unless noted) |
|--------|----------------------------------------|
| 🟢 **Trend** | ADX(15m) > 25, Trend Persistence ≥ 20 min (EMA9 slope > 0), VWAP uncrossed for 30 min, Breakout Success (last 5) ≥ 60% |
| 🟡 **Momentum Burst** | ADX ≥ 18, Breakout Success > 50%, VWAP crosses 2‑3/hr. (Also used if ADX>25 but persistence <20 min) |
| 🟠 **Range** | At least 2 of: ADX<18, VWAP crosses >3/hr, Breakout Success ≤30% |
| 🔴 **Whipsaw** | Everything else (high ADX with DI+/‑ crossing, failed breakouts, low confidence) |

**Expiry override:** after 14:00 → Trend→Momentum Burst; Range→Whipsaw.

### 4.3 Regime Confidence (Simple)

A single percentage (0–100%) derived from the four key metrics equally weighted (ADX strength, persistence, breakout success, VWAP behaviour).  
**Only one threshold matters:**

- **Confidence ≥ 70%** → trade the corresponding strategy.
- **Confidence < 70%** → no new trades.

---

## 5. Signal Handling & Strike Selection

- **One position per underlying at a time.**
- Multiple signals within 60s → pick the best strike via a short **Selection Score** (confidence, liquidity, delta, spread).
- Only strikes with **0.25 ≤ |delta| ≤ 0.85** are eligible.
- Max 2 concurrent positions, different underlyings. Portfolio heat ≤ ₹2,000.

---

## 6. Strategy A – Momentum (Trend / Momentum Burst)

### 6.1 Entry Rules (Minimal & Clean)

The original momentum signal is taken directly, subject **only** to these sanity checks:

1. **Trend alignment** – Longs: `ema_cross_pct > 0` AND `mid_price > vwap`. Shorts: opposite.
2. **Spread check** – `(spread / mid_price) ≤ 0.015`.
3. **Risk check** – `(entry – stop) × lotSize ≤ ₹1,500`. If wider, skip (do not adjust the stop).
4. **Signal freshness** – Cancel if signal older than 30 seconds.
5. **Deep ITM expiry ban** – Skip if delta >0.95 on expiry day.

That’s it. No RSI, no multi‑timeframe candle, no ORB gate after 10:30.

**Why so few?**  
Because the regime detector has already confirmed we’re in a momentum‑friendly environment. The original signal already embeds volume, OI, price action – we don’t need to re‑filter everything.

### 6.2 Execution & Profit Management

- **Entry:** Limit order at signal’s limit price, 60s validity.
- **Stop‑loss:** Stop‑limit order; limit = trigger ± 1%. Retry once, then marketable limit.
- **Trailing stop:** Activates at 0.75R profit, trails by 0.25R (updated every minute).
- **Hard time stop:** `min(12 min, 3 × avg winning hold time)`.
- **GTT disaster stop:** 20% away immediately.
- **No partial exits** (1 lot).

---

## 7. Strategy B – Mean‑Reversion (Range Regime)

Active only when regime = **Range** and confidence ≥ 70%.

### 7.1 Entry Triggers

**Long fade:**  
Spot ≤ ORB Low × 1.002, RSI(5m) < 30, price below VWAP by >0.3%, 5‑min candle has a bullish rejection wick (lower shadow ≥2× body, close > open).  
**Short fade:** opposite at ORB High.

**Volume filter:** current 5‑min volume < 1.2 × average (otherwise possible breakout).

### 7.2 Option Selection

Choose ATM/slightly OTM option (|delta| 0.3–0.5) with tight spread.

### 7.3 Exit & Sizing

- **Target:** Return to VWAP → exit 100%.
- **Stop:** Just beyond ORB boundary (0.2% buffer).
- **Time stop:** 20 min.
- **Risk cap:** `(stop distance × lotSize) ≤ ₹750`, else skip.

---

## 8. Safety Mode (Whipsaw)

- **No new trades.**
- Existing positions managed normally.

---

## 9. Global Risk Controls

- **Daily loss limit:** –3% of account (₹3,000) → stop for day.
- **Profit lock:** at +3% → move loss limit to –1%; at +5% → close all, halt.
- **Portfolio heat:** ≤ ₹2,000 total.
- **Consecutive losses:** After 3, only signals with original confidence >0.65 until a win.

---

## 10. Logging & Improvement

Log: timestamp, symbol, regime, confidence, skip reason (if any), trade outcome, MFE/MAE, hold time, **regime before trade** and **regime after exit**.

**Weekly review:**  
For each regime, calculate Win rate, Profit Factor, Avg R.  
Use this data to **validate** the regime detector, not to add filters prematurely.
